# No automated clinical-correctness gate; the learner is the check

The audience is specialists who challenge feedback that sounds wrong, and the examiner's reasoned reply is one of the most valuable parts of a round. We do not add an automated clinical-correctness judge as a gate. The simulation still scores clinical quality and keeps the scores for comparison over time, but they never block a merge. Region and specialty are set in conversation during setup (default: Iceland/Europe, internal and hospital medicine).
