# Resource Library — vetted continuing-education sources

<!-- Licence: CC BY-NC 4.0, Elias Eythorsson (LICENSE.md of the skill). -->

Recommendations are drawn from this list first, so they are real and current rather
than improvised. When a gap is not well covered here, the session finds a specific,
verified source and appends it under "Added during sessions", so the library grows with
your gaps. Edit freely: add your national guidelines and local resources.

Legend:  **[Free]**  **[Freemium]**  **[Paid]**  **[Subscription/Institutional]**
Tag each entry with blueprint domain codes (CARD, PULM, ID, RENAL, GI, ENDO, HEME,
NEURO, RHEUM, GEN, TOX) or `ALL`.

---

## Core question banks & structured self-assessment
- **ACP MKSAP (19)** — the reference IM self-assessment; MCQ + text. [Paid] — `ALL`
  https://mksap.acponline.org/
- **NEJM Knowledge+** — adaptive IM board review / MOC. [Paid] — `ALL`
  https://knowledgeplus.nejm.org/
- **NEJM Case Records (Case Records of the MGH)** — the CPC series to *read* alongside
  your generated cases. [Subscription] — `ALL`, esp `GEN-10` diagnostic reasoning
  https://www.nejm.org/case-records-of-the-massachusetts-general-hospital

## Journals & point-of-care CME (much is free or institutional)
- **NEJM — Continuing Medical Education** — free article CME on many issues. [Freemium] — `ALL`
  https://www.nejm.org/continuing-medical-education
- **Annals of Internal Medicine — CME/MOC & Point-of-Care CME** — earn credit while
  answering real clinical questions. [Freemium/ACP] — `ALL`
  https://www.acpjournals.org/journal/aim/cme-moc-resources  •  https://www.acpjournals.org/about-poc
- **JAMA Internal Medicine** — high-yield clinical reviews. [Freemium] — `ALL`
  https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamainternalmedicine
- **Medscape Internal Medicine Education** — free multimedia CME. [Free w/ account] — `ALL`
  https://www.medscape.org/internalmedicine/multimedia-cme

## Podcasts (mostly free)
- **The Curbsiders** — case-based IM, very hospitalist-relevant. [Free] — `ALL`
  https://thecurbsiders.com/
- **Core IM** — "Hoofbeats" (diagnostic reasoning), "5 Pearls", "Mind the Gap". [Free] — `ALL`, esp `GEN-10`
  https://www.coreimpodcast.com/
- **IM Reasoning** — clinical reasoning & cognitive bias. [Free] — `GEN-10`, `NEURO`
  (search "IM Reasoning podcast")
- **The Clinical Problem Solvers (CPSolvers)** — schema-driven diagnostic reasoning,
  Virtual Morning Report. [Free] — `GEN-10`, `ALL`
  https://clinicalproblemsolving.com/
- **Annals On Call** — Annals editors discuss key papers. [Free] — `ALL`
  https://annals.org/ (Annals On Call)
- **EMCrit / IBCC (Internet Book of Critical Care)** — resuscitation, sepsis, vent,
  tox. [Free] — `PULM`, `TOX`, `RENAL`, sepsis
  https://emcrit.org/ibcc/toc/

- **UpToDate** — [Subscription/Institutional] — `ALL`  https://www.uptodate.com/
- **IDSA Practice Guidelines** — [Free] — `ID`  https://www.idsociety.org/practice-guideline/practice-guidelines/
- **KDIGO Guidelines** — [Free] — `RENAL`  https://kdigo.org/guidelines/
- **ESC Clinical Practice Guidelines** — [Free] — `CARD`  https://www.escardio.org/Guidelines
- **GOLD (COPD) / GINA (asthma) reports** — [Free] — `PULM`

- **Surviving Sepsis Campaign** — [Free] — sepsis/`PULM`/`ID`  https://www.sccm.org/SurvivingSepsisCampaign/
- **NICE guidance (UK)** — [Free] — `ALL`  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance
- **EASL Clinical Practice Guidelines** — [Free] — `GI`  https://easl.eu/publications/clinical-practice-guidelines/
- **Endocrine Society Clinical Practice Guidelines** — [Free] — `ENDO`  https://www.endocrine.org/clinical-practice-guidelines
- **ESMO Clinical Practice Guidelines** — [Free] — `HEME` (oncology)  https://www.esmo.org/guidelines

## Courses (self-paced / online)
- **SHM (Society of Hospital Medicine) — education & SPARK** — hospitalist-specific. [Paid] — `GEN`, `ALL`
  https://www.hospitalmedicine.org/
- **Harvard Medical School — Hospital Medicine / IM online courses** — [Paid] — `ALL`
  https://cmecatalog.hms.harvard.edu/
- **Mayo Clinic — Online CME catalog** — [Paid] — `ALL`  https://ce.mayo.edu/
- **Coursera / edX clinical courses** — variable; some [Free] to audit — topic-specific

## Conferences (verify dates/location each cycle)
- **ACP Internal Medicine Meeting** (annual) — `ALL`  https://www.acponline.org/meetings-courses
- **SHM Converge** (annual, hospital medicine) — `GEN`, `ALL`  https://shmconverge.hospitalmedicine.org/
- **ESCMID Global (ex-ECCMID)** — `ID`  https://www.escmid.org/
- **ESC Congress** — `CARD`  https://www.escardio.org/
- **National societies** — your national internal medicine society and regional congresses
  often carry locally accredited CME. Add them here.
